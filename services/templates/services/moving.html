THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

A smoother move
starts on paper.

Moving costs and timings depend on volume, distance, access and the work involved. Share these details so the plan can be reviewed before your moving day.

  • Collection and delivery locations in Qatar
  • Property size, furniture and approximate box count
  • Floor levels, stairs, lift access and loading space
  • Preferred moving date and building access windows
  • Packing, dismantling or reassembly requirements
  • Fragile, oversized or specialist items to discuss

HOUSE SHIFTING & OFFICE RELOCATION

Moving your home

For an apartment or villa move, list the rooms and larger items first. Identify belongings you will carry yourself and discuss any items that need extra handling. Keep essential documents and personal items separate from packed boxes.

Moving your workspace

For office relocation, outline desk counts, storage units and access times. Discuss who will handle computers and sensitive documents, and identify furniture that needs dismantling. A clear item list helps define the relocation scope.
